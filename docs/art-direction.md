# BaseWarriors: Meta-Strike Character Art Direction

BaseWarriors uses an original anime-inspired cyberpunk baseball language. Genre conventions are allowed; imitation of a named franchise is not.

## Palette
Deep blue-black surfaces with cyan/fuchsia presentation accents. Character-specific colors come from canonical data and remain stable.

## Lighting
Readable key light, controlled rim light, strong silhouette separation. Effects must not obscure eyes, hair, costume, or equipment.

## Line treatment
Clean anime contour hierarchy with enough contrast for small Telegram/Web surfaces.

## Silhouette and continuity
Hair, eyes, costume, equipment, canonical body preset, role and position are continuity anchors. Variants may change pose, expression, camera and effects, but must not silently redesign the character.

## Framing
Portrait = identity. Card = identity + rarity/role. Battle = readable full silhouette. Expression = emotional state. Cut-in = action/emotion. Presentation = character plus product framing.

## Rarity
Rarity is a presentation treatment, not a redesign of anatomy or costume.

## Quality checklist
character_id consistency; hair/eye/costume consistency; canonical palette; recognizable silhouette; correct framing; no franchise imitation; no unreviewed deformation.
