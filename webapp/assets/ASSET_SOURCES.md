## Important limitation

This inventory records the local SVG files and their intended roles, not a completed external-asset audit. The current inventory does not establish that the local character SVGs are full anime character sprites or that the stadium artwork is a finished baseball stadium. Visual suitability and rendered behavior must be verified against real browser screenshots at the target resolutions.
